Luxury Content Engine - Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

Luxury Content Engine is a personal automation tool used to create and upload videos to the owner's YouTube channel.

Google Account Access

The application requests YouTube upload permission to publish videos to the account authorized by its owner.

Data Usage

Google OAuth credentials and authorization tokens are used solely to authenticate and upload videos. They are not sold or shared with advertisers.

Data Storage and Security

Authorization credentials are stored locally or in private GitHub Actions secrets. They are not published in this website's repository.

Revoking Access

Users can revoke the application's access at https://myaccount.google.com/connections.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the application owner through the contact email shown on the Google OAuth consent screen.
